# Johnson Strategic Solutions — First Paid Written Funding Product (DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW)

**Purpose:** Internal commercial-product specification for the first restricted public release. This is **not published customer copy** and does not authorize billing, financing applications, credit repair, lender contact, or client delivery.

## Product identity

**Name (working):** Business Funding Readiness Blueprint — Up to $250,000 Capital Objective

**What $250,000 means:** A client's *planning target*, never a product price, guaranteed borrowing amount, approved credit limit, or lender commitment. Scope and pricing are quoted in writing **after intake and eligibility review**; do not publish a standard price or pre-charge clients unless approved.

**Service model:** Asynchronous client intake, protected document upload (where authorized), independent analysis, written PDF/XLSX deliverables, and written messaging support. **No consultation sessions.**

**Offer scope:** Business-operations documentation and readiness analysis using verified client-provided evidence. This offer is not financing brokerage, loan application/placement, a credit-repair engagement, accounting/legal/tax services or a promise of funding.

## Mandatory evidence and decision sequence

1. **Use of funds:** Business purpose and 100% itemized capital needs, verified quotes, realistic alternatives.
2. **Amount:** Reconcile every line to the requested target; distinguish requested amount, financeable amount, available equity, and any illustrative gap.
3. **Time horizon:** Planned spend dates, order lead times, quote expiration, lease and contract maturity, asset/useful-life limits.
4. **Repayment source:** Actual tax returns/statements, existing debt, margins, payroll, lease costs, base/downside cash-flow and ability to service obligations. Stop when missing.
5. **Underwriting profile:** Authorized business formation/ownership, credit-readiness information, time in business, documented revenue, cash reserve, collateral/guaranty context. Treat unverified claims as unknowns.
6. **Product:** General education and structurally suitable product categories **only after** prior gates pass; label indicative terms explicitly, never infer approvals.
7. **Lender:** Do not name a lender as eligible or prepare an application until current evidence, program criteria, lawful scope and separate client authorization permit it.

## Seven included written add-ons (scope subject to signed quote)

1. **Source and document index:** Checklist, completeness audit and dated evidence-to-claim mapping.
2. **Financial input register:** Assumptions, sources, missing data and noncash expenses clearly separated.
3. **Scenario stress table:** Downside revenue, labor assumptions and debt service modeled consistently.
4. **Debt and maturity schedule:** Existing/new obligations; lease, contract and obligation-term gaps.
5. **Application rejection preparation:** General documentation deficiency matrix and factual correction routes; no false representations.
6. **Written communications library:** Client-side document requests and vendor/lender inquiry scripts **for the client to use**; no contact on their behalf without separate authorization.
7. **Implementation calendar:** Month-by-month milestones with evidence and stop/go gates, not merely suggestions.

## Required customer-visible disclosures

- **A written readiness service, not a loan offer.** A dollar figure is the client's objective, not approved financing.
- No guaranteed funding, lender approval, debt-service capacity, business performance, credit outcomes, or credit-score changes.
- Every numerical model is illustrative until actual dated financial records are reconciled. Synthetic example data must never be represented as client facts.
- Deliverable does not recommend a particular lender/product until the evidence sequence and separate authorization permit it.
- No consultation calls or sessions. Support and scope clarifications occur through written messages.
- The signed engagement controls actual price, timeline, file formats, permitted data, revision policy, refunds, exclusions and how disputes are resolved.
- Data collection should be minimal; no SSNs, passwords, API keys or authenticator secrets through generic business intake.
- Business advisory must not be represented as consumer credit-repair services or evasion of geographic/legal restrictions.
